TITLE
Healthcare Data Scientist

COMPANY
MediTech Solutions Inc.

REQUIRED_SKILLS
Python, Machine Learning, SQL, data visualization, statistical analysis, familiarity with healthcare regulations (HIPAA)

EXPERIENCE_REQUIRED
Minimum three years of full‑time experience building and deploying data science solutions in a healthcare setting.

RESPONSIBILITIES
- Design, develop, and validate machine learning models to improve patient outcomes and operational efficiency.  
- Clean, preprocess, and analyze large volumes of electronic health record data using Python and SQL.  
- Collaborate with clinicians and product teams to translate model insights into actionable recommendations.  
- Ensure all analyses comply with privacy laws and institutional review board requirements.  
- Communicate technical findings clearly to non‑technical stakeholders through reports and presentations.

QUALIFICATIONS
- Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Statistics, Public Health, or a related field.  
- Proven track record of applying Python, machine learning algorithms, and SQL in real‑world projects.  
- Experience working with healthcare datasets and understanding of clinical terminology.  
- Strong problem‑solving abilities and ability to deliver results under tight timelines.
